BoPS is being constituted as a statutory body under Section 13 of the Merchant Shipping Act, 2025, headed by a Director General under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. The Bureau will oversee ship and port security, deploy CISF-trained personnel, and conduct trial runs at Visakhapatnam, JNPA, and Mundra ports.
