## India hosts 3,682 wild tigers across 55 Tiger Reserves as Project Tiger completes 50 years

Project Tiger completed 50 years of operation, merging administrative oversight with Project Elephant under the National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA). Madhya Pradesh holds the highest tiger population (785 tigers), followed by Karnataka (563), Uttarakhand (560), and Maharashtra (444).

**Core Exam Takeaway:** India is home to 75% of the world's wild tiger population, with tiger numbers estimated at 3,682. Project Tiger was launched on 1 April 1973 at Jim Corbett National Park, Uttarakhand.
