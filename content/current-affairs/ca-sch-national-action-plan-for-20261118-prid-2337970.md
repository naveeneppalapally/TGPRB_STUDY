summary: The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment has implemented interventions for the prevention of substance abuse since 1985-86 pursuant to Article 47 of the Constitution, which directs the State to endeavour to bring about prohibition of intoxicating drinks and drugs injurious to health. Addressing findings from the 2018 National Survey on the Extent and Pattern of Substance Use in India (conducted by NDDTC AIIMS, New Delhi), which revealed over 7 crore people affected by substance use disorders, the National Action Plan for Drug Demand Reduction (NAPDDR) focuses on demand reduction through awareness, counselling, treatment, and combating social stigma.
