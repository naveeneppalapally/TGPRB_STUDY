summary: Indian astronomers from the Indian Institute of Astrophysics (IIA), an autonomous DST institute, along with Gauhati University Ph.D. scholar and INSPIRE Fellow Ali Hasan Sheikh and IIA faculty member Annapurni Subramaniam, discovered a Blue Straggler Star (BSS) caught in active formation in binary system TIC 327546480 within open cluster Collinder 261 (about 9,500 light-years away). Combining NASA's TESS data with ESO's Very Large Telescope in Chile, they observed Roche-lobe mass transfer from a 0.32 solar mass companion onto a 1.67 solar mass BSS.
