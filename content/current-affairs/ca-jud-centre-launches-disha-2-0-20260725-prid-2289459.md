exam_fact: 'DISHA 2.0 (Designing Innovative Solutions for Holistic Access to Justice) is a Central Sector Scheme with Rs 255 crore outlay for 2026-31 comprising four components: Tele-Law (Toll-Free 14454, Nyaya Sahayaks in Aspirational Blocks), Nyaya Bandhu (Pro-Bono under Section 12, Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987; panels in 23 High Courts), LLLAP, and VIDHI-Sanjeevani Integrated Dashboard (targeting 50 lakh beneficiaries) linked with the Nyaya Setu voice-first Hybrid RAG chatbot launched on 29 March 2026 across 22 Indian languages.'
summary: To expand technology-enabled legal empowerment across 2026-31, the Ministry of Law and Justice launched DISHA 2.0 with an outlay of Rs 255 crore. Its four components are Tele-Law (Toll-Free 14454 and Nyaya Sahayaks in Aspirational Blocks), Nyaya Bandhu (pro-bono aid for marginalized groups under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, with panels in 23 High Courts), Legal Literacy and Legal Awareness Programme (LLLAP), and the VIDHI-Sanjeevani Dashboard targeting 50 lakh beneficiaries and integrated with the 22-language Hybrid RAG Nyaya Setu AI chatbot launched on 29 March 2026.
