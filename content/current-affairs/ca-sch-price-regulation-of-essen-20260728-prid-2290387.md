As of 23 July 2026, NPPA has capped ceiling prices for 935 scheduled formulations and retail prices of 3,845 new drugs, enforced a 10% annual MRP increase cap on non-scheduled formulations, and improved access via AMRIT Pharmacy stores offering up to 50% average discounts.
