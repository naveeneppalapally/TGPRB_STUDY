headline: PMMSY Provides Up to 60% Financial Subsidy for Women in Coastal Aquaculture and Blue Economy
exam_fact: Under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY), women beneficiaries are eligible for up to 60% financial assistance (compared to 40% for general category men) for individual and group fisheries activities including seaweed cultivation and modern fish kiosks.
summary: MoS Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying Shri George Kurian informed the Lok Sabha about gender-inclusive provisions in the Blue Economy framework under PMMSY. The scheme mandates an enhanced subsidy of up to 60% of the project unit cost for women entrepreneurs and self-help groups (compared to 40% for general male beneficiaries). Supported enterprises include seaweed cultivation, bivalve farming, ornamental fish breeding, re-circulatory aquaculture systems (RAS), and solar-powered fish vending kiosks.
