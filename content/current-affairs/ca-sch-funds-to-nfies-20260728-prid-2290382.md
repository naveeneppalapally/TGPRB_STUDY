headline: Rs 2,254.43 Crore NFIES Scheme Approves 9 New NFSU Campuses and 7 CFSLs Across India
exam_fact: With an outlay of Rs 2,254.43 crore (2024-25 to 2028-29), the National Forensic Infrastructure Enhancement Scheme (NFIES) funds 9 new National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU) campuses (Rs 1,309.13 crore) and 7 Central Forensic Science Laboratories (Rs 860.3 crore).
summary: MoS Home Shri Bandi Sanjay Kumar informed the Lok Sabha that the Central Sector National Forensic Infrastructure Enhancement Scheme (NFIES) carries a Rs 2,254.43 crore outlay for FY 2024-25 to 2028-29. It allocates Rs 1,309.13 crore for 9 new NFSU campuses (Nagpur, Raipur, Khordha, Chengalpattu, Rajasthan, Bihar, UP, AP, West Bengal) and Rs 860.3 crore for 7 CFSLs (Raipur, Khordha, Chengalpattu, Rajasthan, Bihar, UP, Kerala), with six CFSLs co-located with NFSU campuses.
