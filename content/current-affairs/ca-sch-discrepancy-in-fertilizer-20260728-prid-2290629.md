Dealers contesting a non-standard first lab report may apply within 15 days for second analysis at a National Test House (NTH) centre (Ghaziabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, Chennai, Jaipur); if first and second reports differ on parameters, the third sample goes to Central Fertiliser Quality Control and Training Institute (CFQCTI), Faridabad for final testing.
