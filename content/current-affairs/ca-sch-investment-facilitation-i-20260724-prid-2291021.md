exam_fact: Launched on 29 May 2025, the Ayush Nivesh Saarthi Portal acts as an investment facilitation and market-intelligence dissemination platform consolidating Ayush policy frameworks and incentives for MSMEs and investors, while not directly processing FDI, implementing projects, or tracking export/import transactions.
