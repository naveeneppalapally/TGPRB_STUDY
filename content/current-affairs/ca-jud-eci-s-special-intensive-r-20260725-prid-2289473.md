exam_fact: In its 27 May 2026 judgment in Association for Democratic Reforms & Ors. vs. Election Commission of India & Ors. (W.P.(C) 640 of 2025), the Supreme Court held that ECI's phased Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls-Phase I in Bihar (2025), Phase II across 9 States and 3 UTs (Oct 2025 - Apr 2026), and Phase III in remaining States/UTs including Delhi-is lawful under the Representation of the People Act, 1950 and Article 324.
summary: 'Law Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal outlined the safeguards under ECI''s Special Intensive Revision (SIR) conducted in Phase I (Bihar ahead of 2025 polls), Phase II (9 States and 3 UTs: Chhattisgarh, Goa, Gujarat, Kerala, MP, Rajasthan, TN, UP, WB, A&N Islands, Lakshadweep, Puducherry from Oct 2025 to Apr 2026), and Phase III (remaining States/UTs including Delhi). Governed by the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960 (Forms 6, 7, 8; DM/CEO appeals; BLOs and BLAs), SIR was upheld by the Supreme Court on 27 May 2026 in ADR vs. ECI under the RP Act, 1950 and Article 324.'
