headline: Mission Mausam Deploys BharatFS 6 km Panchayat Model and Integrates AI Models for Weather Forecasting
exam_fact: Under Mission Mausam, the Ministry of Earth Sciences is operationalising the BharatFS high-resolution forecasting model (6 km grid at Panchayat cluster level) alongside GFS (12 km, IMD) and Mithuna-FS (12 km, NCMRWF), while integrating AI-based weather models Pangu Weather, FourCastNet, and GraphCast.
summary: The Ministry of Earth Sciences informed the Lok Sabha on climate modeling enhancements under Mission Mausam. Current operational models include GFS (12 km resolution run by IMD) and Mithuna-FS (12 km run by NCMRWF). Under Mission Mausam, the 6 km resolution BharatFS model will provide localized Panchayat cluster-level forecasts integrated with e-Gramswaraj, Meri Panchayat, and Mausamgram. Additionally, IMD and NCMRWF have integrated state-of-the-art AI/ML atmospheric models including Pangu Weather, FourCastNet, and GraphCast, supported by satellite data from INSAT-3DR, INSAT-3DS, and Oceansat-3.
