headline: PM-Vidyalaxmi Covers 1,425 QHEIs as Higher Education GER Rises From 23.7% to 30.0%
exam_fact: Aligned with NEP 2020 and SDG 4, PM-Vidyalaxmi currently covers 1,425 public and private Quality Higher Educational Institutions (QHEIs) with full interest subvention up to Rs 4.5 lakh income and 3% up to Rs 8 lakh, as India's higher education GER rose from 23.7% (2014-15) to 30.0% (2023-24).
summary: A PIB Backgrounder on the PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme (Cabinet-approved 6 November 2024) noted that India's higher education Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER) climbed from 23.7% in 2014-15 to 30.0% in 2023-24. Supporting NEP 2020 and SDG 4 (2030), the scheme covers 1,425 designated public and private QHEIs for merit-based admissions (excluding management/NRI quotas) and supplements existing full interest subvention up to Rs 4.5 lakh income with 3% subvention up to Rs 8 lakh.
