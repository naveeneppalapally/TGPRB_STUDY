## India adds 5 new wetlands under Ramsar Convention, taking total tally to 85 Ramsar Sites

The 5 new wetlands added include Magadi Kere Conservation Reserve (Karnataka), Ankasamudra Bird Conservation Reserve (Karnataka), Aghanashini Estuary (Karnataka), Karaivetti Bird Sanctuary (Tamil Nadu), and Longwood Shola Reserve Forest (Tamil Nadu). Longwood Shola is an ecologically fragile montane stunted evergreen forest in the Nilgiris.

**Core Exam Takeaway:** India increased its network of Ramsar Sites (Wetlands of International Importance) to 85, placing India 3rd globally in terms of total designated Ramsar sites. Tamil Nadu has the highest number of Ramsar sites (16) followed by Uttar Pradesh (10).
