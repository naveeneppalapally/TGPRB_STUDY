headline: IEPFA and SEBI Organise Eighth Niveshak Shivir in New Delhi for Unclaimed Dividend Settlement
exam_fact: New Delhi became the eighth city to host IEPFA's Niveshak Shivir investor outreach initiative, which began in Pune in 2025 and facilitates direct settlement of unclaimed dividends and shares pending for six to seven years.
summary: The Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (IEPFA), under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, in collaboration with SEBI and Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs), organised a Niveshak Shivir at India Habitat Centre, New Delhi, on 17 November 2026. Dedicated service desks enabled face-to-face settlement of unclaimed dividends and shares pending for six to seven years without intermediaries. Following Pune, Hyderabad, Amritsar, Jaipur, Bengaluru, Bhubaneswar, and Bhopal, New Delhi became the eighth city to host the outreach programme that originally commenced in Pune in 2025.
