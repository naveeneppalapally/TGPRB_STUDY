headline: Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath Becomes India's 45th UNESCO World Heritage Property
exam_fact: At the 48th Session of the World Heritage Committee in Busan, South Korea on 25 July 2026, the 'Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath' (Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh; on Tentative List since 1998) was inscribed as India's 45th UNESCO World Heritage property, comprising Chaukhandi Stupa and the Archaeological Remains of Sarnath (Dhamekh Stupa, Dharmarajika Stupa, Mulagandhakuti Vihara, Ashokan Pillar), known traditionally as Rishipatana, Ishipatana, and Mrigadava where the Buddha delivered his first sermon (Dharmachakra pravartana).
summary: On 25 July 2026, during its 48th Session in Busan, South Korea, UNESCO's World Heritage Committee inscribed India's 2025-26 nomination 'Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath' as India's 45th World Heritage property. Nominated in January 2025 after entering the Tentative List in 1998, the serial property in Varanasi comprises Chaukhandi Stupa and the Archaeological Remains of Sarnath (Dhamekh Stupa, Dharmarajika Stupa, Ashokan Pillar, and vihara remains). Known as Rishipatana, Ishipatana, and Mrigadava (5th-4th c. BCE to 12th c. CE), Sarnath marks the site of Buddha's first sermon (Dharmachakra pravartana), bringing three of four key Buddhist pilgrimage sites (Lumbini, Bodhgaya, Sarnath) onto the World Heritage List.
