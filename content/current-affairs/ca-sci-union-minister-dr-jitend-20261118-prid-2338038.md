summary: Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh unveiled the curtain raiser, official website, and brochure for the 12th edition of the India International Science Festival (IISF) 2026 in New Delhi on 18 November 2026. Organised by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) and Vijnana Bharati (VIBHA), IISF 2026 is scheduled in Pune from 19 to 22 December under the theme 'India's Science, World's Future' (Bharat ka Vigyan, Vishwa ka Bhavishya). Rotating from previous hosts Chandigarh, Hyderabad, Goa, and Delhi, the festival will feature public engagement in frontier science and a special lecture by 2001 Nobel Laureate Sir Tim Hunt.
