Key drivers include 139 First Mile Connectivity (FMC) projects totalling 1,319 MT capacity planned by FY 2029-30 under the Integrated Coal Logistics Plan, allowing ICB plants coal access under the Revised SHAKTI Policy, 2025, and removing the GST compensation cess.
