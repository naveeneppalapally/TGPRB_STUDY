summary: In H1 FY2026-27 (as of 30 September 2026), Public Sector Banks demonstrated robust financial health. Aggregate net profit reached Rs 1.10 lakh crore (16.9% y-o-y growth) and operating profit reached Rs 1.81 lakh crore (13.9% y-o-y). Aggregate business expanded by 15.5% to Rs 301.6 lakh crore, crossing Rs 300 lakh crore for the first time, with deposits reaching Rs 275 lakh crore and advances reaching Rs 136.4 lakh crore. Asset quality showed historic improvement, with Gross NPAs dropping to 1.69% and Net NPAs to 0.33%, supported by a Capital to Risk-weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR) of 16.89%.
