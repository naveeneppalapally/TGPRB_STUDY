exam_fact: Pursuant to Section 18(B)(3) inserted by the MMDR Amendment Act, 2025 (21 August 2025), the Ministry of Coal notified the 'Coal Exchange Rules, 2026' on 4 June 2026 (G.S.R. 442(E)) and designated the Coal Controller Organization (CCO) on 10 December 2025 (S.O. 5730(E)) to register and regulate Coal Exchanges for transparent price discovery.
summary: MoS Coal and Mines Satish Chandra Dubey informed the Rajya Sabha that following Section 18(B)(3) inserted via the MMDR Amendment Act, 2025 (21.08.2025), the Ministry of Coal notified the Coal Exchange Rules, 2026 on 4 June 2026 (G.S.R. 442(E)). Earlier, via S.O. 5730(E) dated 10 December 2025, the Coal Controller Organization (CCO) was appointed Regulator to register Coal Exchanges, approve bidding/price-discovery procedures, and oversee quality-adjusted delivery contracts for captive, commercial, and PSU coal entities.
