headline: ICAR-CISH and APEDA Complete Maiden Commercial Sea Export of 12.5 Tonnes UP Mangoes to Dubai
exam_fact: Using a sea-route export protocol and METWASH shelf-life treatment developed by ICAR-CISH Lucknow and APEDA, 12.5 tonnes of Dashehari and Langra mangoes from Amroha (UP) reached Dubai by sea in 25 days with ~90% quality.
summary: In a first-of-its-kind commercial sea trial reported on 22 July 2026, 12.5 tonnes of Dashehari and Langra mangoes harvested in Amroha, Uttar Pradesh reached Dubai after a 25-day sea voyage in a 40-foot reefer container with ~90% marketable quality. Developed by ICAR-CISH (Lucknow) and APEDA using ICAR-CISH's METWASH treatment, the sea protocol yielded farmers Rs 15-20 per kg extra over conventional exports.
