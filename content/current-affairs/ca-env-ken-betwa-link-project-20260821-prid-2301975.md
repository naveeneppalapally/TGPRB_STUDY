summary: The Ken-Betwa Link Project (KBLP), India's first river interlinking project under the National Perspective Plan, transfers water from the Ken basin to the Betwa basin to alleviate water scarcity in the Bundelkhand region across Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh. With a total sanctioned cost of Rs 44,605 crore, the project includes constructing the 96.7-metre-high Daudhan Dam on the Ken River and a 216.465-km link canal connecting into Barua Sagar near Jhansi, providing irrigation to 10.62 lakh hectares and drinking water to 62 lakh people.
