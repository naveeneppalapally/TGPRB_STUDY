Under JJM 2.0 approved in March 2026, the Mission is extended until December 2028 with total outlay raised to Rs 8.69 lakh crore (central share Rs 3.59 lakh crore) and States preparing Comprehensive Implementation and Reform Plans (CIRPs), alongside the Jal Arpan community handover initiative.
