headline: Over 3.46 Crore SHG Women Enabled as Lakhpati Didis Under DAY-NRLM; SVEP Backs 4.32 Lakh Enterprises
exam_fact: Under DAY-NRLM (implemented across all States/UTs except Chandigarh and Delhi), over 3.46 crore SHG women have become Lakhpati Didis earning at least Rs 1 lakh annually, while SVEP has supported 4.32 lakh non-farm enterprises as of June 2026.
summary: MoS Rural Development Shri Kamlesh Paswan informed the Lok Sabha that DAY-NRLM has mobilised 10.08 crore rural households into 92 lakh SHGs (achieving Rs 13.41 lakh crore bank credit since 2013-14) and enabled over 3.46 crore SHG women as Lakhpati Didis earning at least Rs 1 lakh/year sustainably. Sub-scheme SVEP has backed 4.32 lakh enterprises till June 2026, while SHG products are marketed under registered national brands 'Saras', 'Saras Aajeevika' and 'Aajeevika'.
