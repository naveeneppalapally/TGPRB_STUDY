summary: Replying in the Rajya Sabha, MoS Parliamentary Affairs Dr. L. Funded on a Centrally Sponsored Scheme pattern with Rs 673.94 crore total outlay (Central share limited to Rs 423.60 crore, including Rs 23.58 crore for both Houses of Andhra Pradesh Legislature), NeVA is supported by a Central Project Management Unit (CPMU).
