summary: Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan formally launched the Viksit Bharat - Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) [VB-G RAM G] Yojana from Mukkavaripalli village in Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh alongside CM Chandrababu Naidu and Deputy CM Pawan Kalyan. Replacing MGNREGA's 100-day norm with 125 days of guaranteed work, the scheme targets Rs 1.51 lakh crore expenditure in Year 1 and Rs 7.5 lakh crore over five years across 2.86 lakh panchayats, mandating work within 15 days or an unemployment allowance.
