headline: PMMSY Group Accident Insurance Scheme Enrols 35.74 Lakh Fishers Annually With Enhanced Rs 5 Lakh Cover
exam_fact: Under the Group Accident Insurance Scheme (GAIS) of PMMSY (total investment Rs 20,750 crore), insurance cover was enhanced to Rs 5 lakh for accidental death/permanent total disability, Rs 2.5 lakh for partial disability and Rs 25,000 for hospitalization, enrolling 35.74 lakh fishers annually.
summary: The Department of Fisheries reported that under PMMSY (total outlay Rs 20,750 crore since 2020-21), the Group Accident Insurance Scheme (GAIS) provides Rs 5 lakh for death/permanent total disability, Rs 2.5 lakh for partial disability and Rs 25,000 hospitalization cover, shared 60:40 between Centre and States (90:10 for Himalayan/NE States and 100% Centre for UTs). An average of 35.74 lakh active fishers are enrolled annually.
