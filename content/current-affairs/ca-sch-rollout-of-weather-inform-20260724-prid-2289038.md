Meanwhile, PMFBY's YES-TECH-launched in Kharif 2023 (paddy, wheat) and Kharif 2024 (soybean)-mandates a 30% technology yield weightage across 12 States and 344 districts in 2025-26 (including 100% in MP, 50% in Maharashtra and UP, and 40% in Odisha and Karnataka).
