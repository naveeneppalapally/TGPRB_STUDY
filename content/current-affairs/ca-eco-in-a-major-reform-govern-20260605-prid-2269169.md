summary: Implementing the FY 2026-27 Budget reform via the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-Debt Instruments) (Third Amendment) Rules, 2026, the Ministry of Finance permitted individual Persons Resident Outside India (PROIs) to invest in listed Indian equities under PIS, raising individual limits from 5% to 10% and aggregate limits from 10% to 24%. For FPIs in G-Secs, the Fully Accessible Route (FAR) was expanded to 15, 30, and 40-year G-Secs and Sovereign Green Bonds, while retaining overall caps of 6% (Central G-Secs) and 2% (SGSs).
