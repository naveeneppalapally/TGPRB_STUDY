headline: India Becomes Only Country Running Real-Time 6 km Global Weather Model via Bharat Forecast System

Built by IITM-Pune, NCMRWF-Noida, and IMD and powered by supercomputers Arka and Arunika, BharatFS makes India the only country operating a real-time 6 km global weather model.
