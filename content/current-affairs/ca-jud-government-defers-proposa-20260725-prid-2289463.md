headline: Centre Defers Ninth Schedule Inclusion for Karnataka's 56% Reservation Act Pending High Court Proceedings
exam_fact: The Central Government deferred Karnataka's request to place the Karnataka Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Reservation of Seats in Educational Institutions and of Appointments or Posts in the Services under the State) Act, 2022-which forms part of Karnataka's 56% reservation framework for SC/ST/OBCs-into the Ninth Schedule of the Constitution as the law is sub-judice before the Karnataka High Court.
summary: Law Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal told the Lok Sabha that the Centre decided not to proceed for now with Karnataka's reference seeking inclusion of its 2022 SC/ST Reservation Act (part of the State's 56% reservation framework for SCs, STs, and OBCs) in the Ninth Schedule to the Constitution, because its validity is challenged and sub-judice before the High Court of Karnataka.
