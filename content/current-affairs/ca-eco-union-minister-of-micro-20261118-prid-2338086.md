summary: Union Minister for MSME Jitan Ram Manjhi inaugurated the MSME, KVIC, COIR, and National SC-ST Hub (NSSH) Pavilions at the 45th India International Trade Fair (IITF) at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, under the theme 'MSME-Vikas Bhi, Virasat Bhi'. The pavilion features 437 stalls (including 292 MSME units, 101 KVIC, 34 NSSH, and 10 Coir stalls) representing 29 States and UTs. More than 98 percent of participating MSMEs are first-time exhibitors, with 57.87 percent women entrepreneurs and 27.73 percent SC/ST entrepreneurs, as national MSME registrations crossed 10 crore.
