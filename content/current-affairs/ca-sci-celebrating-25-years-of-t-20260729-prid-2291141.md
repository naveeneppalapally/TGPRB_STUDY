summary: The Indian Institute of Astrophysics (IIA) celebrated 25 years of the 2-metre Himalayan Chandra Telescope (HCT) atop Digpa Ratsa Ri in Hanle, Ladakh (4,517 metres altitude), named after Nobel Laureate Subramanyan Chandrasekhar. Saw First Light on 26 September 2000 and dedicated in August 2001, HCT is operated remotely in real time from Hosakote, Bengaluru via ISRO's INSAT-3B link, offering >250 clear nights annually for optical and near-infrared astronomy (HFOSC, uTIRSPEC, HESP).
