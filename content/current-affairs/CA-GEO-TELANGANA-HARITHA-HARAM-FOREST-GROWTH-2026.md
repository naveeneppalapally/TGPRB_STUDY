## Telangana records 7.7% increase in green cover through large-scale afforestation and Haritha Haram

As per Forest Survey of India and state forest records, Telangana increased its green cover substantially through village nurseries (Palle Prakruthi Vanams), urban parks (Brihat Palle Prakruthi Vanams), and road block plantations. Hyderabad was recognized as 'Tree City of the World' for consecutive years.

**Core Exam Takeaway:** Telangana achieved a 7.7% expansion in total tree and forest cover (increasing from 19,854 sq km to 26,969 sq km), driven by the Telanganaku Haritha Haram initiative and strict protection of Kawal and Amrabad tiger corridors.
