summary: MoS Prime Minister's Office Dr. Jitendra Singh informed the Lok Sabha that the Department of Atomic Energy (DAE) is actively developing indigenous Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) to decarbonize captive industrial clusters and replace retiring coal-fired thermal power plants. Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC) is designing the 220 MWe Bharat Small Modular Reactor (BSMR-200, a pressurized heavy water variant), a twin-unit 55 MWe light water SMR, and a High-Temperature Gas-Cooled Reactor (HTGCR up to 5 MWth). The government aims to operationalise at least five SMRs by 2033.
