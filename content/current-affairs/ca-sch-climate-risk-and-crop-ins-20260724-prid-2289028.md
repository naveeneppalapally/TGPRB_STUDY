headline: SDRF and NDRF Crop Input Subsidy Applies at 33% or Higher Loss Alongside PMFBY Area and Individual Assessment
exam_fact: MoS Agriculture Ramnath Thakur clarified in the Rajya Sabha that PMFBY and RWBCIS apply an Area Approach (via CCE and YES-TECH) generally, and individual farm assessment for localized hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst, and post-harvest cyclone losses, whereas SDRF/NDRF natural-calamity input subsidy is payable only when crop loss is assessed at 33% or more (with IMCT verification for NDRF).
summary: Explaining crop damage compensation norms in the Rajya Sabha, MoS Agriculture Ramnath Thakur stated that PMFBY/RWBCIS rely on an Area Approach using Crop Cutting Experiments (CCE) and YES-TECH for widespread risks while providing individual farm-level assessment for hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst, and post-harvest cyclone losses. By contrast, SDRF/NDRF natural-calamity input subsidy triggers only when crop loss reaches 33% or above (requiring IMCT verification for NDRF).
