summary: GAGAN (GPS Aided GEO Augmented Navigation), India's indigenous Satellite-Based Augmentation System (SBAS) jointly developed by ISRO and the Airports Authority of India (AAI), achieved a major milestone in June 2026 when DGCA conducted India's first satellite-based landing system approach on a commercial jet aircraft. Broadcast through geostationary satellites GSAT-8, GSAT-10, and GSAT-15, GAGAN places India alongside the US, Europe, and Japan with an operational SBAS.
