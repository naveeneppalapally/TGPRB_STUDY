exam_fact: Under Article 222 and the 1998 Memorandum of Procedure (based on the 1993 Second Judges and 1998 Third Judges cases), proposals to transfer a High Court Judge are initiated by the CJI in consultation with four senior-most SC Judges (with no prescribed timeline), while HC appointment proposals under Articles 217 and 224 are initiated by the HC Chief Justice and two senior-most puisne HC Judges at least 6 months before vacancy.
summary: Replying in the Lok Sabha, Law Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal explained the constitutional and MoP framework (1998, flowing from the Second Judges Case of 6 Oct 1993 and Third Judges Advisory Opinion of 28 Oct 1998). Under Article 222, HC Judge transfers are initiated by the CJI with the four senior-most SC Judges after seeking views from the two concerned HC Chief Justices and SC Judges (with no timeline), while HC appointment proposals under Articles 217/224 are initiated by the HC Chief Justice with two senior-most HC Judges at least 6 months ahead of vacancies before scrutiny by the SC Collegium.
