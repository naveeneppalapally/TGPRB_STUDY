summary: MoS Law and Justice Shri Arjun Ram Meghwal informed the Rajya Sabha about indigenous AI tools developed for the Indian judiciary under eCourts Phase III. The AI Division of the National Informatics Centre (NIC) and the Centre of Excellence for eCourts developed 'LegRAA' (Legal Research Analysis Assistant). Trained on Supreme Court, High Court, and district court judgment corpora and central acts, LegRAA provides judicial officers with semantic search, fact-to-precedent matching, and headnote summarisation, while ensuring strict human-in-the-loop judicial oversight.
