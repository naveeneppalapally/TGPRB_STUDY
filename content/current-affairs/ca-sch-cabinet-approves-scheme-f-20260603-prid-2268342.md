summary: The Union Cabinet approved a two-year Rs 9,585-crore scheme funded through the National Capital Region Planning Board (NCRPB) across Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan, and Uttar Pradesh to replace ~2.07 lakh BS-IV or earlier trucks and buses with BS-VI or electric vehicles.
