exam_fact: Introduced on 27 July 2026, the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026 proposes raising individual imprisonment to 5-10 years (fine up to Rs 50 lakh), service-provider debarment to 8 years (fine Rs 5 crore), and adding Sections 12A and 12B for Special Fast Track Courts.
summary: Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh introduced the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026 in the Lok Sabha to strengthen the 2024 Act. The Bill raises penalties for unfair means (5-10 years imprisonment and up to Rs 50 lakh fine), organised crime (7-10 years and up to Rs 10 crore fine), and service providers (up to Rs 5 crore fine and 8-year debarment), empowers referral to a Special Task Force, and adds Sections 12A and 12B mandating 2-month investigation, Special Fast Track Courts for 3-month trials, and 3-month High Court appeal disposal.
