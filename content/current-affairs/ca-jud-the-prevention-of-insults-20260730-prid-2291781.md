headline: 'Parliament Considers Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026: Equal Legal Status for Vande Mataram'
exam_fact: The Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026 seeks to amend Section 3 of the 1971 Act to extend equal penal protection to the National Song 'Vande Mataram' alongside the National Anthem 'Jana Gana Mana' (punishable with imprisonment up to 3 years).
summary: The Rajya Sabha considered the Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026, introduced to amend the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971. The bill amends Section 3 to prohibit intentional disrespect, desecration, or disruption of the singing of the National Song 'Vande Mataram', prescribing imprisonment up to three years or fine or both, identical to penalties for disrespecting the National Anthem. It also introduces Section 3A mandating a minimum one-year prison term for repeat offenders.
