headline: SASTRA Researchers Develop Patented Nanofluid Electrolyte for Rechargeable Zinc-Air Batteries
exam_fact: Researchers at SASTRA Deemed University, Thanjavur, led by Dr. S. Devaraj with DST Nano and Advanced Materials Division support, developed a patented nanofluid electrolyte (Indian patent IN570691) using silica and ZnO nanoparticles in standard electrolyte that remains stable for over 3 months for rechargeable zinc-air batteries, alongside an alpha-MnO2 with 2 wt% Cu catalyst and conversion of spent water-filter carbon to MnO2/C (patent application 202441032753).
summary: With support from the Department of Science and Technology (DST) Nano and Advanced Materials Division, researchers led by Dr. S. Devaraj at SASTRA Deemed University, Thanjavur developed a nanofluid electrolyte by dispersing silica and zinc oxide (ZnO) nanoparticles in standard electrolyte, earning Indian patent IN570691 and showing stability beyond 3 months in rechargeable zinc-air batteries. The team also formulated an alpha-MnO2 catalyst doped with 2 wt% copper and upcycled spent water-filter carbon into MnO2/C (patent application 202441032753) along with surgical mask upcycling.
