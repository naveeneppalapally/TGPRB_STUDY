## MISHTI initiative accelerates mangrove plantation across 9 coastal states and 4 Union Territories

Launched to protect shoreline habitats from cyclonic surges and sea erosion, MISHTI operates through convergence between CAMPA funds, MGNREGS, and private CSR. India holds 3% of South Asia's mangrove cover, with West Bengal (Sundarbans 42.45%) and Gujarat (23.66%) hosting the largest mangrove tracts.

**Core Exam Takeaway:** The MISHTI (Mangrove Initiative for Shoreline Habitats & Tangible Incomes) scheme aims at intensive mangrove afforestation across 540 sq km along India's coastline, utilizing CAMPA funds and MGNREGS convergence.
