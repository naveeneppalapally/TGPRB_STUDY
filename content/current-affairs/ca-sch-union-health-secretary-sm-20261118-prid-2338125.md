summary: Union Health Secretary Punya Salila Srivastava reviewed institutional performance with Executive Directors of New AIIMS under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) on 18 November 2026. Out of 22 approved AIIMS, 18 are functional with IPD/OPD, trauma, super-specialty departments, and diagnostic facilities, while 4 remain under construction or operationalisation. The 18 functional AIIMS operate 14,860 beds and 404 operation theatres, having completed approximately 97.41 lakh OPD registrations, 4.91 lakh IPD admissions, and 629 organ transplants so far in FY 2026-27.
