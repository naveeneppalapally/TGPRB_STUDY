summary: In the Lok Sabha, MoS PMO Dr. Jitendra Singh outlined DAE's self-reliance in nuclear medicine. BRIT also manufactures 18 varieties of cold kits for compounding with Technetium-99m (99mTc) for diagnostic SPECT imaging. In January 2024, DAE operationalized the Radiation Medicine Centre (RMRC) in Kolkata.
