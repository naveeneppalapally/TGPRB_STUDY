summary: A team from the MACS Agharkar Research Institute, Pune (an autonomous DST institute) discovered five new species of script lichens belonging to the genus Allographa (family Graphidaceae) in the Western Ghats using morphology and three-marker DNA sequencing. Named Allographa keralensis, A. nayakae (honouring lichenologist Dr. Sanjeeva Nayaka), A. paraconsanguinea, A. persistinspersa, and A. semicarbonisata, the findings were published in Mycological Progress.
