headline: Commerce Department Amends Export Promotion Mission Interventions to Broaden Support for Exporters
exam_fact: Under the amended guidelines of the Market Access Support intervention of the Export Promotion Mission, the maximum eligible export turnover for airfare reimbursement for trade delegations and fairs has been increased from Rs 75 lakh to Rs 50 crore, with airfare reimbursement enhanced up to 95 percent for Micro and Small Exporters.
summary: 'The Department of Commerce amended guidelines for four interventions under the Niryat Disha sub-scheme of the Export Promotion Mission (EPM) on 17 November 2026: Market Access Support (MAS), Trade Regulations, Accreditation & Compliance Enablement (TRACE), and Logistics Interventions for Freight and Transport (LIFT). Under MAS, airfare reimbursement eligibility turnover rose to Rs 50 crore and council membership requirement was cut from six to three months. Under TRACE, eligible reimbursement certificates expanded from 462 to 600. Under LIFT, low export-intensity coverage expanded from 13 to 34 States and UTs, and minimum distance eligibility between origin and destination ports was reduced from 200 km to 100 km.'
