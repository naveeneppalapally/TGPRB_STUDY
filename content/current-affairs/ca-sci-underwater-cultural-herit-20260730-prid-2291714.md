UAW has conducted offshore diving expeditions, side-scan sonar mapping, and excavations at ancient maritime sites including Dwarka and Bet Dwarka (Gujarat), Mahabalipuram (Tamil Nadu), and Karaikal (Puducherry). Offsite underwater explorations at Poompuhar (Kaveripattinam) have been licensed to the Tamil Nadu State Department of Archaeology.
