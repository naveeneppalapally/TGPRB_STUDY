headline: Asia First Telecom Manufacturing Zone Established in Gwalior with Rs 493 Crore Central Funding
exam_fact: Asia's first integrated Telecom Manufacturing Zone is being established in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, supported by 100 percent Central funding of Rs 493 crore for Phase-I and a Special Purpose Vehicle with 51 percent MP Government equity and 49 percent Department of Telecommunications equity.
summary: A Letter of Intent (LoI) distribution ceremony was scheduled in Bhopal on 19 November 2026 for the development of Asia's first Telecom Manufacturing Zone in Gwalior, attended by MP Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav and Union Minister of Communications Jyotiraditya M. Scindia. The Central Government provides 100 percent funding support of Rs 493 crore for Phase-I, while the Government of Madhya Pradesh provided approximately 170 acres of land free of cost. A Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) has been established with 51 percent equity participation from the Government of Madhya Pradesh and 49 percent from the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) to manufacture mobile devices, optical fibre, and 5G/6G equipment.
