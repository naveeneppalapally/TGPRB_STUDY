Ltd., Indore (Madhya Pradesh) to provide financial assistance for indigenous commercialisation of Functional Soya Protein Concentrates (FSPC) and Textured Soya Protein Concentrates (TSPC). The project uses indigenously sourced non-GMO Indian soybeans with advanced solvent extraction and thermal texturisation to replace imported plant protein concentrates.
