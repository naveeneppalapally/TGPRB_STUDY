headline: Kosi-Mechi Intra-State Link Targeted for March 2029 Under PMKSY-AIBP Alongside Sapta-Kosi Surveys
exam_fact: Included under PMKSY-AIBP with balance cost of Rs 6,143.22 crore (Central share Rs 3,652.56 crore), the Kosi-Mechi Intra-State Link Project in Bihar (8.5% physical progress on Eastern Kosi Main Canal) is slated for completion by March 2029, while the 2003 Indo-Nepal JPO-SKSKI prepares DPRs including GLOF studies for the Sapta-Kosi and Sun-Kosi Kamla Multipurpose Projects.
summary: Replying in the Rajya Sabha, MoS Jal Shakti Raj Bhushan Choudhary reported that the Kosi-Mechi Intra-State Link Project under PMKSY-AIBP (balance works Rs 6,143.22 crore; Central aid Rs 3,652.56 crore) targets completion by March 2029, with 8.5% progress on Eastern Kosi Main Canal remodelling. Separately, the Joint Project Office (JPO-SKSKI, set up 2003) has completed survey, drilling, and GLOF studies for Nepal-linked Sapta-Kosi and Sun-Kosi Kamla Multipurpose Projects, while Bihar received Rs 206.77 crore under FMBAP over three years.
