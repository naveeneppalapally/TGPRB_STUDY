The 6 projects comprise MFL-Madras (Chennai, Tamil Nadu), NFL-Nangal (Punjab), KFL-Shahjahanpur (UP), Matix-Panagarh (West Bengal), CFCL-Gadepan (Rajasthan), and IIPL-Jagdishpur (UP). Each plant will add 12.7 LMT annual production capacity, advancing national self-reliance in fertilizers.
