summary: In a written reply to the Rajya Sabha, MoS Social Justice and Empowerment Ramdas Athawale presented NCRB Crime in India data (2020-2024) regarding the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989. In 2023, cases registered stood at a five-year high of 53,372 (with 45,439 chargesheeted). In 2024, registered cases numbered 48,669 (with 40,614 chargesheeted), while convictions reached a period high of 5,192 and persons convicted reached 10,839 (more than double the 4,855 recorded in 2020). Pending trials stood at 2,87,694 at year-end 2024.
