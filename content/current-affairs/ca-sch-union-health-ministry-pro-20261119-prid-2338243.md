headline: Health Ministry Proposes Inclusion of Antimicrobials as New Drugs Under 2019 Clinical Trials Rules
exam_fact: The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare issued draft notification G.S.R. 983 (E) proposing to amend Rule 2(1)(w)(v) of the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, by including 'antimicrobials' within the definition of 'New Drug'.
summary: Through draft Gazette Notification G.S.R. 983 (E) dated 6 November, the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare proposed amending the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, to classify 'antimicrobials' under the definition of 'New Drug' in Rule 2(1)(w)(v). Initiated in response to concerns over state manufacturing licences granted without bioavailability, bioequivalence, and stability studies, the amendment mandates manufacturers to obtain permission from the Central Licensing Authority (CLA) before applying to State Licensing Authorities (SLAs), strengthening regulatory scrutiny against antimicrobial resistance.
