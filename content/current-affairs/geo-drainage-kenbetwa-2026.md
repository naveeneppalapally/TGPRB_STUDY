# Ken-Betwa River Interlinking Project Update

**July 2026** - The Ken-Betwa Link Project (KBLP), India's first river interlinking project under the National Perspective Plan, continues Phase 1 construction of the Daudhan Dam on the Ken river in Madhya Pradesh.

## Key Points

- **What:** Links surplus Ken river (Yamuna tributary) with deficit Betwa river (also Yamuna tributary)
- **Dam:** Daudhan Dam - 77m height, will create a reservoir submerging parts of Panna Tiger Reserve
- **Beneficiary states:** Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh
- **Irrigation:** Expected to irrigate 10.62 lakh hectares
- **Drinking water:** 62 lakh people in Bundelkhand region
- **Cost:** ₹44,605 crore (Central Government funding)
- **Timeline:** Phase 1 target completion by 2028

River interlinking is a direct PYQ topic (Constable 2018 Mains Q3: "Integration of rivers"). This project connects to:
