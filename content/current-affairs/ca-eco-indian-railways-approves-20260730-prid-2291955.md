exam_fact: Indian Railways approved a Rs 220 crore project to upgrade the Electric Traction System from 1x25 kV to 2x25 kV AT (Auto Transformer) feeding system on the 102 Route Km (204 Track Km) Wadi-Raichur double-line section of South Central Railway.
summary: In a major upgrade for high-density railway corridors, Indian Railways approved a Rs 220 crore project for upgrading the electric traction system on the Wadi-Raichur section of South Central Railway (SCR). The project converts the existing 1x25 kV AC traction to the state-of-the-art 2x25 kV Auto Transformer (AT) feeding system over 102 Route Kilometres (204 Track Kilometres) of double track. This allows the line to carry heavier freight trains, support semi-high-speed passenger operations, and reduce voltage drop.
