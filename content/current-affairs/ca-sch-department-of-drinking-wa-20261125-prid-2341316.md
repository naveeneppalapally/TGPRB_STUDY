headline: DDWS and DGT Sign MoU for Nal Jal Mitra Skilling Programme to Train 1 Lakh Youth Under JJM 2.0

summary: The Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation (DDWS), Ministry of Jal Shakti, and the Directorate General of Training (DGT), Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE), signed an MoU for the Water Supply Operation and Maintenance Skilling Programme (Nal Jal Mitra) under Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0 (JJM 2.0). Signed in the presence of Union Jal Shakti Minister C. R. Patil and MoS Skill Development Jayant Chaudhary, the Rs 111.93 crore programme aims to train 1,00,000 rural youth across Gram Panchayats in plumbing, electrical work, and pump operation to ensure sustainable rural water infrastructure.
