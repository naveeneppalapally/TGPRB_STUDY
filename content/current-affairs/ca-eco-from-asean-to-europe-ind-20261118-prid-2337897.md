summary: According to export performance data for April-September FY 2026-27, India's exports to key FTA partners and major markets witnessed broad-based expansion. Under the CECA framework, exports to ASEAN rose 58 percent from US$ 17.75 billion to US$ 28.06 billion, driven by a 103 percent surge in exports to Singapore reaching US$ 11.07 billion and an 83 percent rise in shipments to Malaysia reaching US$ 5.58 billion. Exports to SAFTA partners grew 22 percent to US$ 15.29 billion, with Sri Lanka surging 73 percent to US$ 3.94 billion and Bangladesh remaining the largest SAFTA destination at US$ 5.66 billion.
