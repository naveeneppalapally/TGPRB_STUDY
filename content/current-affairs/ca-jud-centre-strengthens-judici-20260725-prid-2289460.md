For FY 2026-27, Mother Sanctions of Rs 5.33 crore (Karnataka) and Rs 5.195 crore (Himachal Pradesh) were issued, alongside eCourts Phase III funds of Rs 151.73 crore and Rs 20.66 crore respectively.
