## Nagar Van Yojana sanctions 1,000 urban forests across India to combat urban heat islands

Nagar Van Yojana was launched on World Environment Day to create green lungs in urban landscapes. Each Nagar Van covers a minimum area of 10 hectares up to 50 hectares, fostering biodiversity, recreational spaces, and carbon sequestration.

**Core Exam Takeaway:** The Nagar Van Yojana aims to create 1,000 Nagar Vans (City Forests) and Nagar Vatikas (City Gardens) on degraded urban/peri-urban lands across the country using CAMPA (Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority) funds.
