exam_fact: The Department of Telecommunications (DoT) and the Government of Madhya Pradesh signed an MoU to establish India's first dedicated Telecom Manufacturing Zone (TMZ) at Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, to manufacture 5G/6G radio equipment and optical components.
summary: In the presence of Union Communications Minister Shri Jyotiraditya M. Scindia, the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) and the State Government of Madhya Pradesh signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to set up a state-of-the-art Telecom Manufacturing Zone (TMZ) in Gwalior. Spread across dedicated industrial acreage with plug-and-play testing facilities, the TMZ will anchor domestic manufacturers of 5G/6G Massive MIMO antennas, routers, optical fibre amplifiers, and satellite ground transceivers, leveraging PLI scheme incentives.
