summary: The Union Cabinet chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi approved a revised Policy for Award of Waterfront and Associated Land to Port Dependent Industries (PDI) in Major Ports (Captive Policy). Modernizing the erstwhile 2016 policy, the new framework permits existing captive berth operators (power, steel, fertilizer, and petrochemical units) to expand handling capacity by building new jetties, deep-water terminals, and Single Buoy Moorings (SBMs). The policy standardizes concession periods up to 30 years, mandates competitive bidding, and rationalizes royalty sharing on surplus cargo.
