## India's total forest and tree cover reaches 24.62% of geographical area

The Forest Survey of India (FSI), Dehradun releases the biennial India State of Forest Report (ISFR). As per latest findings, the top 5 states by forest area are Madhya Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, and Maharashtra. By percentage of geographical area, the top states are Mizoram (84.53%), Arunachal Pradesh (79.33%), and Meghalaya (76.00%). Haryana has the lowest forest cover in India.

**Core Exam Takeaway:** India's total forest and tree cover stands at 8,09,537 sq km (24.62% of total geographical area), against the 33% National Forest Policy 1988 target. Madhya Pradesh has the largest area under forest cover, while Mizoram leads in percentage (84.53%).
