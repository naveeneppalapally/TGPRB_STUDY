headline: 700-Year-Old Banyan in Bihar's Munger Identified as Oldest Accurately Dated Banyan Tree
exam_fact: Scientists from BSIP Lucknow accurately dated a ~700-year-old banyan tree (Ficus benghalensis) in Munger, Bihar using AMS radiocarbon dating, making it the oldest accurately dated banyan tree.
summary: Scientists led by Dr. Trina Bose from the Birbal Sahni Institute of Palaeosciences (BSIP), Lucknow, an autonomous DST institute, dated a banyan tree (Ficus benghalensis) in Munger, Bihar to around 700 years old, making it the oldest accurately dated banyan tree. Published in Quaternary Research, the team used Accelerator Mass Spectrometry (AMS) radiocarbon dating on alpha-cellulose extracted from near the pith.
