summary: MoS Chemicals and Fertilizers Smt. Anupriya Patel informed the Lok Sabha that the Central Government approved a grant-in-aid of Rs 1,000 crore for establishing Common Infrastructure Facilities (CIF) in the Bulk Drug Park at Haroli, Una district, Himachal Pradesh. Selected as one of three national Bulk Drug Parks (alongside Gujarat and Andhra Pradesh), the project incorporates a 5 MLD Common Effluent Treatment Plant (CETP) with Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD), dedicated steam generation, captive power lines, and solvent recovery plants to slash domestic API production costs.
