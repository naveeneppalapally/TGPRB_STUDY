By building over 60,000 recharge structures (including 500+ defunct borewells converted into recharge shafts and 600+ injection wells linked to 1,700+ mini percolation tanks), geo-tagging 2,500+ water bodies, and shifting nearly 70% of summer paddy to lower-water crops, the district recorded an average groundwater rise of ~2.04 metres.'
