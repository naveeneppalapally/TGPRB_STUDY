summary: The Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare issued Draft Gazette Notification G.S.R. No. 987 (E) dated 2 November 2026 proposing amendments to the Drugs Rules, 1945. The amendment introduces a separate application form (Form 19-BC) and licence form (Form 20-BC) for the wholesale sale of bulk drugs (active or inactive bulk substances), bifurcating bulk drugs from formulations. The reform was approved by the Drugs Consultative Committee (DCC) and Drugs Technical Advisory Board (DTAB) to strengthen supply chain oversight and control the illicit movement of NDPS-notified substances and antimicrobials.
