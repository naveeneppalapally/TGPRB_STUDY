headline: Government Supports Changpa Nomadic Tribe in Ladakh for Pashmina Wool Production Under PMJVM
exam_fact: Under the Pradhan Mantri Janjatiya Vikas Mission (PMJVM), the Ministry of Tribal Affairs supports the Changpa nomadic pastoralist tribe of Changthang (Ladakh) through Pashmina wool value addition, solar sheds, and Van Dhan Vikas Kendras (e.g. VDVK Durbuk sanctioned with Rs 15 lakh).
summary: In response to a parliamentary question on the impact of climate change on the Changpa tribe in Ladakh, the Ministry of Tribal Affairs outlined targeted support under PMJVM. The high-altitude nomadic pastoralist Changpa community of the Changthang plateau produces fine Pashmina (Cashmere) wool from Changthangi (Pashmina) goats. Interventions include subsidized predator-proof solar livestock corrals, winter feed stocking, and financial support to Van Dhan Vikas Kendras such as VDVK Durbuk (sanctioned Rs 15 lakh) for dehairing and textile processing.
