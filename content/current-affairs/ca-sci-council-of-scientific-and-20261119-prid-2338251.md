summary: The Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) held a technology transfer and MoU signing event in New Delhi on 19 November 2026 chaired by DG CSIR Dr. N. Kalaiselvi. CSIR-SERC Chennai transferred its Emergency Retrieval System (ERS) for rapid restoration of power transmission lines to IFAB Global, and its 100% Recycled Aggregate bricks and Ultra-High-Performance Concrete (UHPC) pavement technology to NATRAX. Additionally, CSIR-CRRI transferred MicroReAct-RAP cold micro-surfacing technology to three industrial partners and joined CSIR-IIP Dehradun to transfer Bio-Bitumen produced from agricultural residue (rice straw/parali).
