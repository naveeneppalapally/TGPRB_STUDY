## Scope and evidence standard

Read the full live Note, its 31 linked canonical PYQs, all 16 Study sections, 18 deck cards, five gate questions, five advanced drills and the legacy Markdown companion. This is a factual correction and provenance pass, not a certification of every historical answer key or existing image. No official question wording, options, answer indices, question IDs, card IDs, section IDs, personal-note anchors or FSRS scheduling rules are changed. No unrelated topic is rewritten. Canonical explanation edits propagate to other surfaces sharing those same question IDs.

Sources support only their listed claims. Government hosting does not make a legacy state table current. For example, Karnataka's Godavari page still groups Telangana under undivided Andhra Pradesh; use its evidence for the Karnataka catchment, not as a post-2014 state count.
